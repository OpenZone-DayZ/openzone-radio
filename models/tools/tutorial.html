the radio

Six models on one frequency grid. The only difference is range: it is in the radio's description.

Tuning the frequency

The window is the face of the radio in your hands. Click its buttons with the mouse. The keyboard stays with the game: you can run with the window open. Close it with K, Esc or the cross in the corner. Drag the window by the radio body; the place is remembered.

Screen

On a radio with digits, the top line is the frequency it is on, the bottom line is what you are typing. Sheptun and Bazar show the channel number big and its frequency small.

The dot is inserted for you

Type 1455, and the screen shows 145.5. Confirm, and the radio tunes to 145.500.

Off the grid

Frequencies go in steps of 0.05 MHz. If the frequency is out of the band, the window says “Outside this set's band”. Type it again.

Talking

the lock

The lock: press T twice quickly and the radio keeps transmitting hands-free. Any next press releases the lock. Do not forget it: an open channel gives the whole group away.

Which radio talks

Only one radio transmits, the first on the list:

  1. in your hands
  2. on the backpack strap
  3. in the backpack or a pocket (if the server allows it)

If two radios are in the same place, the one you held last talks.

Who hears you

Every radio that is on, tuned to the same frequency and within reach.

Any radio that is on receives, even at the bottom of a backpack. A radio that is off hears nothing.

The range is in the radio's description: from 250 m on the Sheptun to 10 km on the Crystal.

136–156 MHz

Frequencies and channels

All radios share one band: 136.000–155.950 MHz in steps of 0.05, that is 400 channels. Any two radios can tune to the same frequency. Sheptun and Bazar show the channel number, so agree on this scale:

136.000 · channel 1 channel N = 136.000 + 0.05 × (N − 1) · every 20 channels = 1 MHz channel 400 · 155.950
good to know

Your own radio key

Settings → Controls → OpenZone → “Radio: push to talk”. After you rejoin, the new key drives both your voice and the air, while Caps Lock stays voice-only nearby.

A dropped radio

Drop the radio while holding T and it goes silent. Drop it on the lock and it keeps transmitting. Death releases the lock by itself.

Battery and rejoining

A dead battery means the radio is silent and deaf, and the lock is released. A radio that was in your hands is off after you rejoin: turn it on again.
